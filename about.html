ABOUT US

Built by Students, for Students

We are Shivansh, Simon and Harshit a team of passionate students with a shared interest in technology, innovation, and web development. We believe in using technology to solve real-life problems and make everyday tasks easier and more efficient.


Our project, AI Study Planner, is an initiative to make student life more organized and productive. Through this project, we aim to create a smart and user-friendly platform that helps students plan their studies, manage their tasks, and stay focused on their academic goals.

Working together on this project has allowed us to apply our technical knowledge, improve our problem-solving skills, and explore new ways to build useful digital solutions.

We are continuously learning, experimenting, and growing as developers, with a vision to turn creative ideas into meaningful technological solutions.

Our Vision: To learn, innovate, and build technology that makes a difference.

Three minds, one vision — building smarter solutions for students.
